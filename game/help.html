Controls ▼

Use Arrow Keys (↑ ↓ ← →) to move your character.
Press SPACE or click mouse to shoot enemies.

Enemies ▼

Enemies come in different speeds and attack styles.
Some chase you, others attack from distance.

Weapons ▼

Upgrade bullets into black flames for higher damage.
Faster attacks increase survival chances.

Tips & Tricks ▼

Keep moving constantly.
Learn enemy patterns.
Dodge first, attack second.
